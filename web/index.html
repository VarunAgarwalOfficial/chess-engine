Chess

Play chess against an engine, a program that chooses its own moves, running in this page. In a new game you have the white pieces, at the bottom of the board: make the first move by dragging a piece, or by tapping it and then the square it should go to.

Yellow-tinted squares show the last move, green the piece you picked up, and grey dots where it can go. The letters under the board and the numbers beside it name each square, as in e4; moves are written that way.

    Accuracy Inaccuracies ?! Mistakes ? Blunders ??

    Accuracy is how much of the winning chance a side's moves kept: 100% is the engine's choice every time.

    The strip below is White's winning chance through the game: the light part is White's share. Dots mark the inaccuracies, mistakes and blunders; click it to jump to a move.

    More: a new game, saved games, the position as text, the board editor and board settings

    To draw arrows, right-click and drag; on a touch screen press and hold an empty square, or use Draw arrows. Keys: the arrow keys step through the moves, F flips the board, M turns sound on or off.

    Board editor

    Pick a piece, then click squares to place it. Drag pieces to move them, or off the board to remove them.
